Referrals

Refer a Vulnerable Adult

We accept referrals from local authorities, probation services, NHS, adult social care, domestic abuse services, and partner agencies across Devon.

Referral Partners

Who Can Refer?

🏛️

Local Authority Housing Teams

Torbay Council Housing Needs Team, Housing Options, and homelessness prevention services can refer individuals directly through our spot-purchase pathway.

⚖️

Probation Services

Probation officers can refer prison leavers and individuals on community orders who need stable accommodation linked to compliance requirements.

🏥

NHS & Adult Social Care

Mental health teams, addiction services, hospital discharge teams, and adult social care can refer individuals who need supported housing as part of a care pathway.

🛡️

Domestic Abuse Services

Partner agencies such as FearFree and TDAS can refer survivors who need safe, confidential accommodation with structured support.

🤝

Voluntary & Community Sector

Recovery organisations, charities, and community groups can refer individuals they're supporting who need accommodation alongside continued engagement.

👤

Self-Referrals

Individuals can also approach us directly. We will assess their needs and, where appropriate, work with them and relevant agencies to arrange a placement.

How It Works

The Referral Process

1

Initial Contact

Referrer contacts us by phone or email with basic details about the individual and their circumstances.

2

Assessment

We conduct a risk assessment and needs assessment to determine suitability and match the individual to an available placement.

3

Placement & Induction

The resident moves in, completes their induction, and is assigned a dedicated key-worker. Housing Benefit claims are submitted immediately.

4

Support & Review

Ongoing key-worker sessions, regular support plan reviews, and multi-agency liaison to ensure the placement is working and progress is being made.

Eligibility

Who Is Eligible?

Aged 18+

Our service is for adults only. We do not accommodate families with children or unaccompanied minors.

Eligible for Housing Benefit

Residents must be eligible for Housing Benefit or Universal Credit housing element to fund their placement.

Willing to Engage With Support

Residents must be willing to engage with their key-worker, attend scheduled sessions, and work towards the goals in their support plan.

Assessed as Suitable

All referrals are subject to a risk and needs assessment. We will work with referrers to determine suitability and manage any identified risks.
